For a bug, please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of More).

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone and, if iCloud sync is on (More › iCloud sync), in your own iCloud account. The developer can't see it.

How do I move to a new iPhone? With iCloud sync on, sign in with the same Apple Account and open the app. Or use More › Import & export › Export backup, then Import backup on the new phone.

Can I import my spreadsheet? Yes. Choose Import a spreadsheet on the welcome screen, or More › Import & export › Import spreadsheet, and pick an Excel, Numbers or CSV file.

The Lock Screen card disappeared. iOS ends Lock Screen cards after about 8 hours. Opening Wealthy-ish brings it back.

How do I delete everything? More › Import & export › Erase all data removes your data from this iPhone and from iCloud.

Wealthy-ish is for tracking and planning. It is not financial advice.
