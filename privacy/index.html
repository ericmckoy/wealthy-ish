Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Wealthy-ish ("the app") is a personal net-worth tracker. This policy covers the iOS app.

The short version

Wealthy-ish does not collect, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

What the app stores and where

  • On your device. The accounts, balances, cash flow, goals and settings you enter are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • In your iCloud account (optional). With iCloud sync on (Settings in the app: More › iCloud sync), the same data is stored in Apple's iCloud key-value store under your Apple ID so your devices stay in sync. Apple handles this storage under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

What the app does not do

  • It never connects to banks, brokerages or other financial services, and never asks for account passwords.
  • If you connect Apple Wallet, it reads the balances of the Apple Card, Apple Cash or Savings accounts you choose through Apple’s FinanceKit, on your iPhone only. Those balances are stored with the rest of your data (on device and, if on, your iCloud) and are never sent to the developer or any third party. You can turn access off in Settings at any time.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It makes no network requests to the developer or any third party.

Face ID / Touch ID

If you turn on the app lock, iOS verifies your face, fingerprint or passcode. The app only receives whether the check succeeded. Biometric data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave.

Calendar and Reminders

If you turn on the Lock Screen agenda card, the app reads your calendars' events for the next 30 days and your unfinished reminders, and shows today's and the next upcoming events and reminders on your Lock Screen. They are read on your device each time you open the app and are never stored, synced or sent anywhere. You can turn the card off in More › Widgets, and remove access in iOS Settings.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show figures from your data (for example your net worth). The app shares them with its widgets through storage on your iPhone only.

Notifications

The optional monthly reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No push service is used.

Exports

When you export a backup or spreadsheet, the file goes wherever you choose in the iOS share sheet. The app keeps no copy beyond a temporary file in its cache.

Deleting your data

More › Import & export › Erase all data removes everything from the device and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
